About me

Hello! I am Yiming Cui (崔一鸣), a Ph.D. student in the College of Transportation at Tongji University, advised by Prof. Jian Sun. I received my B.Eng. in Transportation Engineering from Tongji University in 2023.

My research focuses on end-to-end autonomous driving, including efficient training methods and closed-loop training and testing environments. I also study vehicle-to-vehicle and vehicle-to-infrastructure cooperation in complex traffic scenarios.
